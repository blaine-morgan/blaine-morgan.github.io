What I build

Web apps for the jobs that are still done by hand.

They work on a phone in the field, a tablet at the counter and a desktop in the office. Built around your workflow, not a template.

  • Intake and requests

    Customers or staff submit once. It lands in the right queue with everything attached.

  • Quotes and approvals

    Draft on site, send from the phone, approved with a tap, straight onto the schedule.

  • Scheduling and dispatch

    Who is where, what is next, what changed. Everyone sees the same board.

  • Checks and inventory

    Counts, inspections and sign-offs recorded where they happen, not retyped later.

  • Status boards

    The answer to "where is it" without the phone call. For the team and, when you want, for the customer.

  • Reports that write themselves

    The weekly numbers assembled from the work as it happens, not from Friday afternoon's spreadsheet.

Why it costs less than you expect

One builder, an automated build pipeline, and a scope that stays small on purpose.

I run an in-house build system that handles the repetitive parts of writing and testing software. A working app for one task does not need a team or a quarter.

Every project starts with a single task, the one you would hand off first. That keeps the first version quick to build, quick to learn and quick to pay for itself. The next task is a decision you make after, not before.

Questions

The things people ask before they scan the card.

What happens to my answers?

They are used to build your demo and, if you ask for one, your prototype. The demo itself runs on sample data. Nothing is shared or sold.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The demo, the prototype and the finished app all run in a browser, on a phone, tablet or computer.

What if the demo gets it wrong?

Tell it. The feedback step exists because a first guess from four questions is a starting point, not the answer. What you correct is what the prototype is built from.

How long does it take?

The demo takes about a minute. A prototype for a single, well-described task follows within days. The real app is scoped from the prototype, so the timeline is a number, not a guess.

What does it cost?

The demo and the feedback step are free. Price comes with the scope, after the prototype, when we both know exactly what is being built.
